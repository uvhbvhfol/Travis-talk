Point to or say a letter. When Travis nods YES, tap that letter. The message updates instantly.

Caregiver: explain that YES confirms the letter being shown; NO skips that letter. Only press a button after you clearly see his response. Stop if he's tired or the response is uncertain.
How it works: Use Tap letters to directly add a letter after Travis confirms it. In Spell a message, it asks one predicted letter at a time, most likely first. The most likely next letter is asked first, using common English letter patterns and the word being spelled. Each NO moves to the next most likely letter. Word suggestions can save even more answers. Space, Delete, and Speak are available as separate buttons. In Quick needs mode, the same system identifies a common phrase.
Designed as a free, private communication aid. No account, camera, microphone, network connection, or AI service is required. Not a medical device or replacement for clinical communication assessment.
